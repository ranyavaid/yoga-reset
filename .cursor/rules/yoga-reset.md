# Yoga Reset Cursor Rules

This project is a calm mudra-based micro-reset app.

## Product direction

- The app should feel calm, minimal, editorial, and ritual-like.

- Avoid game-like, productivity, dashboard, or achievement-based UI.

- The experience is about guided breathing and hand mudra practice.

## Technical rules

- Do not rewrite MediaPipe tracking unless explicitly asked.

- Do not rebuild the app architecture unless explicitly asked.

- Preserve webcam, timer, breathing, and mudra detection logic.

- Make small targeted changes.

## Visual rules

- Use soft frosted glass cards.

- Use warm cream/off-white surfaces.

- Use subtle borders, rounded corners, and calm spacing.

- Do not generate mudra illustrations in code.

- Always use local illustration assets from `/public/illustrations`.

## Current scope

- Focus on one-hand Vayu Mudra first.

- Two-hand tracking is future scope.

- Keep ambient sound lightweight and optional.

## Language

Use:

- “practice”

- “reset”

- “hand alignment”

- “breathe in”

- “breathe out”

Avoid:

- “puppet”

- “challenge”

- “score”

- “win”

- “success”
