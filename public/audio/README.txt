Ambient practice audio
========================

Yoga Reset loads optional looping beds from this folder:

- rain.mp3   — Rainfall
- river.mp3  — River Flow
- birds.mp3  — Morning Birds

The in-app **Soundscape** control chooses a track (or Off). Nothing plays until you
change that control — there is no autoplay on page load.

Keep files gentle, loopable, and free of loud transients; the player peaks around 20% volume.
